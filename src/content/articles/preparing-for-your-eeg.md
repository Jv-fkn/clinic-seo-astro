A good recording depends on clean contact between the sensors and your scalp. A few simple steps make a real difference.

## The night before

Wash your hair with ordinary shampoo and let it dry naturally. Do not use conditioner, oils, gels or hairspray, because they create a barrier between the sensors and your skin.

If you are booked for a [sleep-deprived EEG](/services/sleep-deprived-eeg), follow the sleep instructions in your appointment letter.

## On the day

Eat as normal. Low blood sugar can change the recording. Bring your referral letter, a list of your medications and anything that helps you relax.

## After the test

The paste washes out with warm water. Most people return to normal activities straight away. Results go to your referring doctor, and our [guide to EEG results](/articles/eeg-results-explained) explains what the report contains.
