Your EEG report is written for your doctor, so the language can feel technical. Here is what each part usually covers.

## Background activity

This describes your main brain rhythm while awake and relaxed. A steady rhythm of around 8 to 12 cycles per second at the back of the head is typical in adults.

## Findings during activation

The report notes any changes during deep breathing, flashing lights or sleep. Some patterns only appear under these conditions.

## Interpretation

The final section summarises whether the recording is within normal limits and how any findings relate to the reason you were referred.

## A normal result is useful, not final

Many people with epilepsy have a normal EEG between seizures. That is why your doctor may suggest a longer [ambulatory EEG](/services/ambulatory-eeg) if your symptoms continue.

Questions worth asking at your follow-up: Does this change my diagnosis? Do I need further testing? Does anything change in my treatment?
